## Google Analytics (Only with Your Consent)

If you agree in the consent banner, this site uses Google Analytics 4, a web analytics service of Google Ireland Limited, Gordon House, Barrow Street, Dublin 4, Ireland ("Google").
I use it to see which articles are read and how visitors find the site.

Google Analytics sets cookies (`_ga`, `_ga_<ID>`) that store a pseudonymous identifier for up to two years.
It processes, among other things, the pages you visit, the time spent on them, the referring website, your browser, operating system, screen size, and your approximate location derived from your IP address.
According to Google, IP addresses are not logged or stored in Google Analytics 4.
The data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA; Google LLC is certified under the EU–U.S. Data Privacy Framework.

The legal basis is your consent according to Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR and § 25(1) TDDDG.
Without your consent, Google Analytics is not loaded and no request is sent to Google.
If your browser sends a "Do Not Track" or "Global Privacy Control" signal, this is treated as a refusal.

You can withdraw your consent at any time with effect for the future: use the link "Privacy settings" at the bottom of every page and choose "Decline".
Google Analytics is then switched off and its cookies are deleted.

Details: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) and [Google Analytics data practices](https://support.google.com/analytics/answer/6004245).

## Storage of Your Consent Choice

Your choice in the consent banner ("Accept" or "Decline") is saved in your browser's local storage under the key `fk-consent-analytics`, so that the banner does not appear on every page.
This value stays in your browser and is not sent to me or to anyone else.
It is stored until you change your choice or delete your browser data.

Storing this value is strictly necessary to respect your choice (§ 25(2) No. 2 TDDDG); the legal basis is Art. 6(1)(c) GDPR in conjunction with Art. 7(1) GDPR, which requires me to be able to demonstrate consent.

## YouTube Videos

Some articles contain YouTube videos.
They are shown as a placeholder, and nothing is loaded from YouTube until you click play.
Only after your click is the player loaded from youtube-nocookie.com, a service of Google Ireland Limited.
From then on, YouTube receives your IP address and technical data about your browser, and may set cookies or use similar technologies; data may be transferred to Google LLC in the USA.

The legal basis is your consent by clicking play (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR, § 25(1) TDDDG).
Alternatively, you can use the link "Watch on YouTube" below each video.
Details: [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
